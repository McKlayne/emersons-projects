Emerson's Process for How to Do Research

1 Choose a Topic
Select a subject that interests you and that you want to learn more about.
2 Define or Narrow the Topic
Refine your topic to make it more specific and manageable for research.
3 Ask Questions
Develop questions that will guide your research and help you discover what you need to know.
4 Start Searching
a Start with a prayer
b Start with your brain
c Use books
d Use technology
e Interview people
5 Store Notes
Keep organized records of your findings, sources, and insights as you research.
Use a notebook or journal to write down key points and quotes
Create digital notes in apps like Evernote, OneNote, or Notion
Use index cards to organize information by topic or source
Keep a research log with dates, sources, and page numbers
Highlight and annotate important passages in books or articles
Create a bibliography as you go to track all your sources
6 Synthesize, or Put It Together
Combine and organize your research findings to create a coherent understanding of your topic.
Create an outline organizing your main points and supporting evidence
Write a summary paragraph connecting different sources and ideas
Make a mind map or concept map showing relationships between ideas
Develop a thesis statement that captures your main conclusion
Create a timeline or flowchart if your topic involves a process or history
Compare and contrast different viewpoints you discovered
Identify patterns, themes, or common threads across your sources
7 Share Your Research with Others
Present your findings and insights to others, contributing to collective knowledge.
Write a research paper or essay explaining your findings
Give a presentation or speech to your class or group
Create a poster or visual display of your research
Start a blog or website to share your insights
Participate in a discussion or debate about your topic
Teach what you learned to someone else
Create a video or podcast explaining your research
Present at a conference or research symposium
8 Give Thanks to God
By prayer and telling Him thank you for giving more light and knowledge, or anyone who helps you.
